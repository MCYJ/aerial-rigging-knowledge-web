A few useful distinctions.

Is this an official qualification app?

No. This is an independent study companion, not affiliated with City & Guilds/NPTC or HSE.

Does a completed study session establish competence?

No. Question practice and personal notes do not replace practical assessment, training or site-specific professional judgement.

Where are notebook versions kept?

On the device. The notebook has local Save, comparison and Load; it does not provide cloud sync or an export feature.

What does Q351 mean?

It identifies the position of a study entry in this app. It is not an official examination question number.

Can I download the app now?

The app is being prepared for release. This page does not announce App Store availability or a release date.
